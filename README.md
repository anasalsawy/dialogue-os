## Related research

Dialogue OS also hosts experimental research that is **non-normative** and does not alter the Dialogue OS Constitution unless explicitly incorporated in a future version.

### [Dual-Lobe Interdependent Agent Architecture](research/dual-lobe/README.md)

An experimental cognitive architecture for reliable autonomous agents. It divides one task identity across two functionally specialized model processes—**Strategist** and **Executor**—and tests whether hard tool asymmetry, permission locks, independent verification, constrained communication, durable recovery, and evidence-backed completion reduce false success and unsafe side effects.

The research track also explores optional neuroscience-inspired engineering motifs including bounded cross-module communication, attentional gating, prediction error, episodic replay, action selection, global broadcast, bounded working memory, and adaptive control. These are explicitly presented as **functional analogies and testable engineering hypotheses**, not a literal model of the brain or a claim of machine consciousness.

**Research status:** public experimental specification; empirical evaluation pending.
